- **Engine:** rough idle, misfire, check engine light, hard start, stalling, overheating
- **Transmission:** slipping, harsh shifts, delayed engagement, fluid leak, whining
- **Brakes:** squeal, grind, pedal vibration, pull to side, soft pedal, warning light
- **Suspension/Steering:** clunk, wander, uneven tire wear, bounce, noise over bumps
- **Electrical:** dead battery, dim lights, accessory failure, parasitic draw, blown fuses
- **Exhaust:** loud noise, smell, rattling, failed emissions, smoke
- **Cooling:** leak, overheating, low coolant, sweet smell, steam
