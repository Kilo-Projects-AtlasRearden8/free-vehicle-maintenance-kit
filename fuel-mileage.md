**How to Calculate:**

- **Trip Miles** = Current Odometer − Previous Odometer
- **MPG** = Trip Miles ÷ Gallons (US)  
  **km/L** = Trip km ÷ Liters (metric)
- **Cost / Mile** = Total Cost ÷ Trip Miles

**Tip:** Reset trip odometer at each fill-up, or subtract previous odometer reading. Log partial fills too—just note "partial" in Notes.
